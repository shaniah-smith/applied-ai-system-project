# Mood and Activity Notes

## happy
Happy songs usually have high valence (they sound positive), major keys, and medium to high energy. Good for mornings, getting ready, and boosting your mood.

## chill
Chill songs have low energy and a relaxed pace. They are good background music for studying, reading, or unwinding, especially when they are mostly instrumental.

## focused
Focus music works best when it is steady, repetitive, and has few or no lyrics, because words compete with your attention. Lofi, ambient, and classical are common picks.

## intense
Intense songs have very high energy, loud drums, and fast tempos. They fit workouts and moments when you want to feel pumped up.

## relaxed
Relaxed songs are slow to medium tempo and warm-sounding. They fit cooking, a slow morning, or a calm evening.

## moody
Moody songs sit between sad and calm, often with minor keys and atmospheric sounds. They fit late nights and thinking time.

## sad
Sad songs usually have low valence (they sound negative or bittersweet) and slower tempos. Some people want sad music to match their feelings, which is healthy, but a recommender should not push only sad music to someone all the time.

## confident
Confident songs have strong beats and bold vocals. They are good for hyping yourself up before something important.

## peaceful
Peaceful songs are very calm, low energy, and often instrumental. They are good for sleep, meditation, or winding down.

## angry
Angry songs are aggressive and very high energy. Some listeners use them to release frustration safely, like during a hard workout.

## romantic
Romantic songs are warm and smooth, usually medium-low energy with soulful vocals. Good for date nights.

## nostalgic
Nostalgic songs remind people of the past, often with older production styles or storytelling lyrics. Good for road trips and reflecting.

## euphoric
Euphoric songs feel like a huge rush of joy, with very high energy and big builds. Common at parties and festivals.

## studying
For studying, pick low to medium energy (around 0.3 to 0.5), high instrumentalness, and low speechiness. Lyrics and sudden loud changes make it harder to concentrate.

## workout
For workouts, pick high energy (0.8 and above), strong beats, and fast tempos. Intense, euphoric, and angry moods all work.

## sleep
For sleep, pick very low energy (below 0.3), peaceful or chill moods, and acoustic or ambient sounds.
