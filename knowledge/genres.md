# Genre Notes

## pop
Pop is built around catchy hooks, clear vocals, and a steady beat. It is usually upbeat and easy to sing along to, which makes it a common pick for happy moods, parties, and getting ready to go out.

## indie pop
Indie pop mixes pop melodies with a lighter, more homemade sound. It tends to feel bright and a little dreamy, and it works well when someone wants something happy but less polished than mainstream pop.

## lofi
Lofi (low fidelity) uses soft beats, warm crackly textures, and mostly instrumental loops. It is popular for studying, focusing, and relaxing because it stays in the background without words pulling your attention away.

## rock
Rock is driven by electric guitars, bass, and live drums. It ranges from steady to very intense, and high-energy rock is a common choice for workouts, driving, or letting off steam.

## ambient
Ambient music focuses on atmosphere instead of beats or melodies. It is slow, spacious, and very calm, which makes it good for sleep, meditation, or deep focus.

## jazz
Jazz uses swing rhythms, improvisation, and instruments like piano, sax, and upright bass. Mellow jazz fits relaxed settings like a coffee shop, cooking, or a slow evening.

## synthwave
Synthwave is electronic music inspired by 1980s movie soundtracks, with big synths and drum machines. It often feels nostalgic and moody, and it is a classic choice for night drives.

## hip-hop
Hip-hop is built on strong beats and rhythmic vocals (rap). It often feels confident and bold, and the lyrics are a big part of the experience, so it can be distracting for focused work.

## classical
Classical music is mostly orchestral or solo instrumental pieces like piano. Slow classical pieces are peaceful and help with focus because there are no lyrics.

## metal
Metal uses heavy distorted guitars, fast drums, and aggressive vocals. It is extremely high energy and is often chosen for intense workouts or when someone feels angry.

## r&b
R&B (rhythm and blues) combines smooth vocals with soulful grooves. It often feels warm and romantic, and it suits slow evenings or date nights.

## country
Country music tells stories with guitars, fiddles, and clear vocals. It often feels nostalgic and warm, with songs about home, family, and the past.

## edm
EDM (electronic dance music) is made for dancing, with big drops, fast tempos, and electronic sounds. It is very high energy and euphoric, a common pick for parties and the gym.

## reggae
Reggae has a laid-back offbeat rhythm and a warm, sunny feel. It is relaxed but still groovy, good for summer days or winding down.

## indie
Indie covers artists outside the big labels, often with a raw or emotional sound. Slower indie songs can feel sad or reflective, which suits rainy days and quiet moments.
